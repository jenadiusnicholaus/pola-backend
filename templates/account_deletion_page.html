Pola App — Request Account Deletion

Pola is a legal services platform connecting citizens with legal professionals in Tanzania. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data using this page.

How to Delete Your Account

You can request account deletion in two ways:

Option 1: In the App

  1. Open the Pola app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.
  3. Review the list of data that will be deleted and confirm by tapping "Delete My Account".
  4. Your account will be deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period.
  5. To cancel within the 30 days, simply log back in and tap "Cancel Deletion".
