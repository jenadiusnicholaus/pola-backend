How to Delete Your Account

Follow these steps to request permanent deletion of your Pola account and all associated data:

  1. Open the Pola app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account.
  3. Review the list of data that will be deleted and confirm by tapping "Delete My Account".
  4. Your account will be deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period.
  5. To cancel within the 30 days, simply log back in and tap "Cancel Deletion".

Retention Period: After account deletion is requested, your data is permanently removed within 30 days, except for payment records (kept 90 days) and security audit logs (kept 180 days) as required by Tanzanian financial regulations.

Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can request deletion of specific data categories while keeping your account active.

  1. Open the Pola app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Manage My Data.
  3. Select the data categories you want to delete.
  4. Tap "Delete Selected Data" to confirm.
