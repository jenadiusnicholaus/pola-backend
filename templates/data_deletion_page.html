Pola App — Request Data Deletion

Pola is a legal services platform connecting citizens with legal professionals in Tanzania. You can request deletion of specific data without deleting your account.

How to Delete Your Data

You can request data deletion in two ways:

Option 1: In the App

  1. Open the Pola app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy → Manage My Data.
  3. Select the data categories you want to delete.
  4. Tap "Delete Selected Data" to confirm.

Option 2: Using This Page (No App Required)

If you no longer have access to the app, enter your registered email and select the data categories you want deleted:

What Happens After Deletion

CategoryWhat's DeletedAccount Status
Profile InformationPhoto, bio, address, contactAccount remains active
Uploaded DocumentsAll uploaded filesAccount remains active
Call HistoryAll call recordsAccount remains active
Messages & ChatAll conversationsAccount remains active
Location DataGPS coordinates clearedAccount remains active
Device InformationDevice records removedAccount remains active

Note: Your account stays active when you delete specific data. Only the selected categories are removed. Payment records are retained for 90 days for legal compliance.
